    General Booking

    Calculate when general railway reservation opens.

    How it works

    • Booking opens 60 days before the journey date, not counting the journey date.
    • Online booking opens at 08:00 AM IST.
    • Some trains have a shorter booking period.
    • On the opening day, only Aadhaar-verified IRCTC users can book online until midnight.

      Tatkal

      Tatkal Opening Time

      Booking uses the train’s originating-station departure date.

      How it works

      Tatkal opens one day before the origin departure date.

      AC classes 2A, 3A, CC, EC, 3E
      10:00 AM IST
      Non-AC classes SL, 2S, FC
      11:00 AM IST

      Online Tatkal needs an Aadhaar-verified IRCTC account and an OTP. Agents cannot book in the first 30 minutes.

      1A is not listed under the standard Tatkal classes, so no Tatkal time is calculated for it.

              Railway Rules

              The rules every calculation here is based on.

              • Opening dates are calculated from the train’s originating-station departure date.
              • The standard advance reservation period is 60 days.
              • On the opening day, only Aadhaar-authenticated users can book online until midnight.
              • Tatkal opens one day before the origin departure date.
              • Online Tatkal needs Aadhaar authentication and an OTP.
              • 1A is not listed under the standard Tatkal classes.
              • Refunds follow the 2026 refund slabs.
              • Vande Bharat Sleeper and Amrit Bharat II have their own refund scale.
              • First charts follow the Railway Board letter of December 2025.

              Sources: IRCTC cancellation rules (opens in a new tab), 2026 refund slabs (All India Radio) (opens in a new tab), Commercial Circular 08 of 2026 (opens in a new tab), Aadhaar booking rule (All India Radio) (opens in a new tab).
